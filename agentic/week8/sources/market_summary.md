# California Residential Market Summary

The validated residential market dataset covers January 2024 through June 2026. It contains 366,856 deduplicated closed-sale events and 547,263 new-listing events. The data is aggregated before it is used as a RAG source; no addresses, listing identifiers, or row-level MLS records are included.

In January 2024, the statewide median close price was $720,000, average Days on Market was 29.8, and the average close-to-original-list ratio was 99.04%. By April 2026, the median close price was $800,000, average Days on Market was 23.6, and the ratio was 99.46%.

The source-quality audit flags January, May, and June 2026 for unusually low new-listing volume and May 2026 for unusually low closed-sales volume. Values from those months must be described as potentially incomplete source periods rather than confirmed market collapses or spikes.
