# Real Estate Data Analyst Primer - Core Terminology

DOM means Days on Market. It measures the number of days between a property's listing date and the date it goes under contract or is removed from active marketing, depending on the MLS definition. Lower DOM generally indicates faster market absorption; comparisons should use the same status and date rules.

The list-to-close ratio compares the final close price with a listing price. In this project, the close-to-original-list ratio is ClosePrice divided by OriginalListPrice. A ratio of 1.00, or 100%, means the property closed at its original asking price; above 100% means it closed above the original asking price, and below 100% means it closed below it.

A comp is a recently sold property used as evidence when assessing the likely market value of another property. Strong comps are similar in location, property type, size, condition, and sale date.

Escrow is the neutral process in which funds and documents are held while the parties complete the conditions required for a real-estate transaction.

Cap rate is net operating income divided by property value or purchase price. It is mainly used for income-producing property and is not the same as appreciation or cash-on-cash return.
