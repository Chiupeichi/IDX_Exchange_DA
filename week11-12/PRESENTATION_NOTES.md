# Five-Minute Presentation Notes

## Slide 1 — Irvine market intelligence

Today I will present the final market intelligence findings for Irvine in
Orange County. This analysis uses the cleaned Residential data from the earlier
weeks and the two Tableau workbooks completed for Weeks 8–10.

## Slide 2 — Softer pricing and a slower sale cycle

April 2026 is the latest month with complete listing and closed-sale activity.
The median close price was 1.36 million dollars, which was 4.2 percent lower
than April 2025. Homes took an average of 24.8 days to sell, about 4.4 days
longer than a year earlier. The average close-to-original-list ratio was 98.2
percent, so sellers were generally accepting slightly less than the original
asking price.

## Slide 3 — Price trend

The monthly median price was volatile rather than steadily declining. There
were several rebounds during the last 12 complete months, but April finished
below the year-ago benchmark. The median price per square foot was 768 dollars,
down 5.5 percent year over year. This supports the conclusion that unit pricing
also softened and that the lower median was not only caused by a change in home
size.

## Slide 4 — Market activity

Activity gives a more balanced picture. April new listings increased 8.2
percent to 330, and closed sales increased 11 percent to 131. However, trailing
12-month sales volume was about 2.1 billion dollars, 5.7 percent below the prior
12 months. The latest month improved, but the broader market was still cooler.

## Slide 5 — Competitive landscape

Cesi Pagano was the leading listing agent during the trailing 12-month period,
with about 102.5 million dollars in volume and 72 units. Coldwell Banker Realty
was the leading office, with about 149.2 million dollars and 108 units. Its
share was only 7.2 percent of total Irvine volume, so no single brokerage
dominated the market.

## Slide 6 — Final takeaway

Overall, Irvine is becoming more balanced, not inactive. Pricing and market
speed moved in buyers' favor, while April transaction activity remained
resilient. Sellers should price close to current comparable sales and plan for
a longer marketing period. Buyers have more negotiating room, but well-priced
homes are still selling. The Tableau dashboards allow the same findings to be
explored by month, location, ZIP code, and property subtype.
